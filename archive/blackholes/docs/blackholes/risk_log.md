## Superradiance / active band risk
- Kerr superradiant bands can show amplification (effective |R(omega)| > 1) by extracting spin energy.
- This does not violate passivity; it changes the energy ledger relative to the hole's free-energy budget.
- In a cavity (barrier reflection + inner reflection), round-trip gain > 1 can trigger an instability
  ("BH bomb" or cavity gain > 1).
- In our interface-EFT, the non-superradiant inequality |R| <= 1 is replaced by a ledger-aware constraint.
- The BH-11 toy demo shows growth when |R0*r| > 1; detailed Kerr dynamics are deferred.
