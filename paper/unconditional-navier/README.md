# Separate research manuscript

This draft is a new project, not a revision of paper/main.tex or the sibling
conditional theorem. It makes no unconditional closure claim. Its working
target, proof obligations and evidence records are kept outside this repository.

Compile from this directory with `latexmk -pdf -outdir=build main.tex`.
Figures enter only with explicit original-domain and error/limit statements.
Any theorem-completion claim requires an independent proof review first.

The round67 pressure-source control rules out the stated norm-only
instantaneous differential closure on genuine original local solutions with
fixed initial energy and critical mass. Its role is a scoped strategy decision;
the all-data source and unconditional target remain open.
