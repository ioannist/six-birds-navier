\section{Version note}\label[appendix]{app:version}

Version~1 of this preprint appeared on 4 March 2026. Version~2 (14 October 2026) follows a
mathematical review of the supporting derivations, Lean library and numerical code. Its main
changes are:
\begin{itemize}
\item the bridge is defined with sharp Fourier shells, with the sign of the supplied power fixed, and
an exact return on strong solutions is proved (\cref{prop:bridge-return});
\item a section on what energy alone gives the capacity is added, including the counterexample to
a shell-only strain bound (\cref{sec:energy-capacity});
\item the no-go theorem is stated on a feasible class, and the continuum passage is listed as open;
\item the divergence comparisons are stated with the correct quantifiers, and the work-quantum
lemma states its regularity assumptions;
\item the route-capacity theorem is connected to Lean through tame-factor adapters, and the
anti-localization lemma through a Parseval identity; the theorem index lists all 31 audited
declarations;
\item the residual-charged anti-localization bound and the regularized-model appendix are added;
\item the time stepping, de-aliasing, sign conventions and diagnostics of the numerical code are
corrected, and all figures are regenerated; the illegal example's concentration factor is
$\approx404$ in the corrected code.
\end{itemize}
The title and the main mechanized theorems are unchanged.
