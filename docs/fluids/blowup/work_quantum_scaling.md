# Work quantum and capacity comparisons

The supplied power p_j=<u_j,y_j>, positive work W_j⁺=∫p_j⁺, and actual
bridge are fixed in [ns_bridge_object.md](ns_bridge_object.md). A useful
frontier must link actual PDE regularity loss to finite crossing times.
That return is OPEN and cannot be inferred from arbitrary thresholds.

## Two proposed frontiers and their unpaid adapters

1. Positive flux activation: A(t)=∫_{t_j}^t Π_j⁺, crossing a threshold θ_j.
   To yield work quantum θ_j, an independent comparison of that actual
   flux with the SGS supplied work is required.
2. Weighted shell activation: A(t)=sup_{τ∈[t_j,t]}2^{2s(j+1)}||u_{j+1}(τ)||₂².
   An absolute value may already exceed its threshold at t_j, giving an
   immediate zero-duration crossing. For fresh work, use an increase from
   the value at t_j and prove a storage/work inequality. A threshold alone
   never proves W_j⁺≥θ_j.

For the continuous storage-growth frontier with passivity, the attained
threshold does supply W_j⁺≥θ; its precise assumptions and proof are in
[scale_passivity_axioms.md](scale_passivity_axioms.md).

## Valid scaling conclusion

An independent energy-only **absolute** CAP choice is
C2^{5j/2}||u0||₂³ ([cap_bound_attempt.md](cap_bound_attempt.md)). With that
choice and a constant or polynomial work quantum, the comparison series
Σw(j)/Cap(j) converges. It therefore cannot certify No-Zeno. A sharper
capacity could have divergent reciprocal sum; an upper bound on capacity
alone cannot exclude that. Nor does failure to construct a work quantum
prove that every sufficient work quantum requires a strain estimate.

WORK, a useful CAP/DIV pair, and the actual frontier/continuation return
remain separate open obligations. No new source attempt is asserted.
